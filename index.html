Counting Rods

Draw any number the way Chinese mathematicians did on their counting boards: bundles of rods, upright and flat in turn. SVG with zero dependencies, plus PNG, JPEG and WebP.

Try any number

The playground draws whatever you type, breaks it into base-10 digits, and lets you download SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Zero, fractions and negatives

Zero is a circle or a gap, a dot marks the decimal point, and negatives get a diagonal rod or black rods. See how.

Zero dependencies

@counting-rods/core turns a number into an SVG string in any JavaScript runtime.

Images and CLI

@counting-rods/raster renders PNG, JPEG and WebP with sharp, and @counting-rods/cli does it from the terminal.
